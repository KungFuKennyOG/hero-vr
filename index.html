Hero / Villain

An open city and your powers. What you do with them decides who you are.

On Quest 3, open this page in the Meta Quest Browser and press Enter VR.

Quest controls
Trigger
Fly where that hand points. Both hands = full speed.
Grip
Telekinesis: point at a car, crate or person and squeeze. Swing and let go to throw.
Right stick ↑↓
Push or pull what you're holding. ←→ snap turn.
Left stick
Walk.
A / X
Super jump.
B / Y
Dive. Hit the ground fast for a shockwave slam.
Desktop controls
Mouse
Look (click the game to capture the mouse).
WASD
Walk, or steer while flying.
Space
Fly where you look. Hold Shift too for double thrust.
Right mouse
Telekinesis grab. Move the mouse to swing it, release to drop or fling.
Left mouse
While holding: hurl it forward.
Wheel
Push or pull what you're holding.
E / C
Super jump / dive and slam.
